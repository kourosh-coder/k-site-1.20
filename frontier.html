Frontier

Destroy the enemy HQ (top right). Hold mines for income and build the right army.

Soldier: 6 HP, move 2. Archer: 4 HP, range 2. Knight: 8 HP, move 3. Soldier beats Knight, Knight beats Archer, Archer beats Soldier (+2 damage). Mines give +3 gold per turn while you hold them; step on one to capture it. New units can act next turn.
